How ShiftFloor works

For facilities

  1. Sign up and add your first location.
  2. Post a shift — role type, pay, headcount, and required certification.
  3. Watch applicants come in, or turn on auto-accept for instant fills.
  4. Close out the shift and rate the workers who showed up.

For workers

  1. Sign up and upload your certifications for verification.
  2. Browse shifts ranked by your certs, distance, and pay.
  3. Claim a shift in one tap — get confirmed instantly or once the facility approves.
  4. Show up, get paid, and build your rating for better matches next time.
