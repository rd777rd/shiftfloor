  • Forklift — Class I through VII

  • OSHA 30-Hour General Industry

How verification works

Upload a photo or PDF of your certification during signup or from your profile. Our team reviews it and marks it Verified, Pending, or Rejected — you can only claim shifts requiring a certification once it's Verified.
